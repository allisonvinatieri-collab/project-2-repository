# Costco business-quality memo

**Verdict: own the business.**

Costco produced positive owner earnings in every reported fiscal year, rising from $3,501 million to $9,382 million. Return on equity stayed above the required threshold in every year, ranging from 25.1% to 31.2%. Net income also rose from $5,844 million to $9,226 million. These results indicate a business that earns well on its equity and converts operations into cash after capital spending.

**Decision:** I trust this verdict because the conclusion uses only repeatable SEC 10-K calculations, although it is a judgment of business quality rather than a recommendation on price.
